Watch X videos in a window you can resize

Paste a public post or video link from x.com or twitter.com. Drag any edge of this window — the player is not stuck in a fixed layout.

  1. Copy a post URL from X.
  2. Click Paste or drop it in the bar and press Open.
  3. Optional: Sign in with your own X account for private or restricted videos. The session stays on this computer.
  4. Pin with Always on top if you want the player above other windows.

Keyboard: ⌘/Ctrl+L focus link · ⌘/Ctrl+O open clipboard · ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+T always on top · F1 help · F11 hide toolbar

X Video Window

A small desktop player for X (Twitter) videos. Load a post in an embedded browser, resize freely, and optionally keep the window on top.

  • Links: x.com and twitter.com status, video, and /i/status URLs.
  • Sign in: use the Sign in button and log into your X account. Credentials are never scraped; cookies stay in this app’s session.
  • Focus: the focus toggle hides X’s sidebar, left nav, and reply thread so the video is easier to watch.
  • Private videos: stay signed in. Closing the app does not log you out.

This app does not bypass X login or download videos from CDNs.

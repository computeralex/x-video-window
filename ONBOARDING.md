# Quick start — Unofficial 𝕏 (Twitter) Video Liberator

Watch X videos in a window you can actually resize. No Share Extension or Shortcuts setup required.

1. **Open the app.** On a Mac, use the packaged `.dmg` when you have one. For development: `npm install` then `npm start`.
2. **Copy a link** to a post, video, or broadcast from X in your browser.
3. **Open video** and paste, or press **⌘/Ctrl+O** to load the clipboard.
4. **Resize** any edge — the video fills the window. Move to the top to show Open video, Sign in, pin, fullscreen, and Focus.
5. **Sign in** (dedicated window) only if the video needs your X account.
6. **Fullscreen** with the corners button; **Esc** exits.

You can also drag a link onto the window, or open a `xvw:` URL.
